How it works

Three steps, and two of them are one tap.

  1. You ask.Type the amount and what it is for. You get a link.
  2. They pay.They open the link, long-press the code, and pay in WeChat or Alipay. No account, no app, no sign-up.
  3. You are paid.The money settles to your own bank account in your own currency.

What it costs

2%

Taken from the amount received.

The person paying is charged the amount they owe and nothing more. Network and currency-conversion charges are set by the payment provider and shown before anything is sent.

Who it is for

People, not companies.

Tutors, translators, consultants, designers, writers, agents, small studios and sole traders outside China with clients inside it.

Ordinary payment services are built for registered businesses with a website and a merchant account. Most of the people doing this work have neither, and their alternative is a bank branch, a form, and several days of not knowing.

What Dealio does not do

It never holds your money.

  • Payments are processed and funds are held by a licensed payment provider. Dealio is not a bank and is not a money transmitter.
  • Each recipient is identity-checked by that provider, and the money settles to their own account. It does not pass through a Dealio balance.
  • No cash. No cryptocurrency. No bearer instruments.
  • No goods trade, no marketplace, no escrow. Payment for services between two people who already know each other.
  • It is not a way around anybody's reporting requirements or limits. The provider's own limits and checks apply to every payment.
  • The service is not encrypted end to end and does not claim to be.
