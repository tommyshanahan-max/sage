      One person means one browser. Counting is a random id this site puts in that browser's own storage — no account, no IP address kept, no third party, nothing that follows anyone anywhere else. The same person on a phone and a laptop is two; someone who clears their storage is new again.

      Days are not added up. The bars are people per day, and the same person on Monday and Tuesday is one person, not two — so there is no honest weekly total to show and none is shown. Pages and functions are summed over the range, because those are actions, and two of them are two.

      Nothing here is finer than a day. The browser's id is hashed before it touches disk, and no time of day is written next to it — so these files record how many people came, never when any one of them was here.
