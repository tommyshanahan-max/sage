Your apps

Instagram, WhatsApp and the rest live inside the browser, not here. Both refuse to be embedded in another page, and a link from here would load from wherever you are rather than from Tokyo — which is the whole point. Bookmark them in the browser instead.

The figure above is a real HTTP round trip to the server, measured from this device, not a ping, and it counts only what runs in Tokyo. Sign-in is required on every service; a green dot means the machine answered, not that you're logged in.

Tiles under Your apps are hosted elsewhere and open from wherever you are, so their dots answer a different question — whether this device can reach that app at all. A red one there is worth knowing: it means the app is unreachable on this connection, and the browser in Tokyo is the way round it.
