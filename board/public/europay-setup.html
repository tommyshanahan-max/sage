Europay · setting up

Putting Europay on your own domain.

Your payment page lives at an address you own — pay.yourcompany.com — wearing your name and your colours. It runs on our servers. You are not installing software, hosting anything, or maintaining anything. You point one name at us and we do the rest.

Everything we need from you is on this page. It takes about ten minutes, most of which is waiting.

What we need from you

1

The address you want it on

A subdomain of a domain you already own. Most people use pay. — pay.yourcompany.com. It can be anything: china., invoice., checkout..

Tell us the exact hostname. We set it up our end before you change anything.

2

One DNS record

Once we confirm we are ready, add a single CNAME record wherever your domain's DNS is managed — the registrar you bought it from, or Cloudflare, or your host's control panel.

TypeCNAME
Name / Hostpay
Value / Targeteuropay.paydealio.com
TTL300 (or Automatic)
Proxy / CDNOFF — see below

That is the whole change. Nothing else on your domain is touched, and your website, email and existing records all keep working exactly as they do now.

3

Your name and two colours

The page wears your brand, not ours.

  • The name as it should be printed — the one your clients know.
  • A deep colour, as a hex code, for the header and the pay button.
  • A paper colour if you want one, also hex. Leave it out and we use a soft off-white.

Two colours, not ten. If you have a brand guide, send the page with the hex codes on it and we will pick.

4

Connect your Stripe

One authorisation, on Stripe's own website, with your own Stripe login. We never see your password and no account is created on your behalf.

Charges are raised on your account. Your name is on every statement, the money settles to your bank, and you can revoke the connection from your Stripe dashboard at any moment.

5

An email address for us to reach you

One human who can answer a question about a payment. Not a shared inbox nobody reads.

Two things that catch people out

Turn the proxy off

If your DNS is on Cloudflare, the record must be DNS only — the grey cloud, not the orange one. With the proxy on, the security certificate cannot be issued and the page will show a warning to your clients. You can turn it back on later if you want to; tell us first.

Do not add the record until we say we are ready

If the name points at us before your side is configured, visitors get a certificate error rather than a page. We will tell you the moment it is safe, and the certificate is issued automatically within a few minutes of the record appearing.

What the record actually connects

What reaches our server

  • Requests to pay.yourcompany.com and pages beneath it.
  • The payment requests you create and what your clients pay.
  • Nothing else, because nothing else is pointed at us.

What does not

  • Your website, shop, blog or admin — different names, your own machines.
  • Your email. MX records are untouched.
  • Your database, your files, your customer list.
  • Your site's logins and sessions — a different hostname is a different origin, so a browser will not hand your cookies to us.

One honest exception

If your site sets cookies on the whole domain — Domain=.yourcompany.com rather than on www — a browser sends those to every subdomain, this one included. Most sites do not. If yours does and you would rather it did not reach us, use a separate domain for the payment page instead and we will set that up the same way.

What you do not have to do

  • No software to install, on your site or anywhere else.
  • No code to paste into your pages.
  • No certificate to buy, renew or configure — we issue and renew it.
  • No server, no hosting, no updates. The page is served from our machines.
  • No change to your existing checkout. It keeps taking cards exactly as it does today.

What happens next

We configure the hostname, then email you to say go. You add the record. Within about ten minutes the address is live with a valid certificate, in your colours, and you can send your first payment request that afternoon.

Europay is payment software. Money never passes through us — payments are processed and held by a licensed payment provider and settle to your own account.
