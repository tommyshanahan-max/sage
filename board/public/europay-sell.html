How it works

Three steps, two of them one tap.

1

You ask.

Type the amount and what it is for. You get a link.

They open it, long-press the code and pay in WeChat or Alipay. No account, no app, no sign-up.

You are paid.

Euros settle to your own bank account, usually inside two days.

Who it is for

People and small firms, not corporates.

Consultants, designers, translators, teachers, agencies, studios and suppliers across Europe, with clients inside China.

What you already have

A bank account, a company number or none at all, and a phone. That is the whole requirement.

What you were told you needed

A Chinese entity, a merchant account, an ICP filing and a finance person. Ordinary payment services are built for that. Most people doing this work have none of it.

What Europay does not do

It never holds your money.

  • Payments are processed and funds held by a licensed payment provider. Europay is not a bank and is not a money transmitter.
  • Each recipient is identity-checked by that provider, and money settles to their own account. It does not pass through a Europay balance.
  • No cash. No cryptocurrency. No bearer instruments.
  • No goods trade, no marketplace, no escrow. Payment for services between two parties who already know each other.
  • It is not a way around anybody's reporting requirements or limits. The provider's own limits and checks apply to every payment.
