What it does

  1. Talk

    Press the disc, speak English, and it says the Chinese out loud — and the other way back. The direction is worked out from what you said, so there is no toggle to hunt for while you are holding a phone across a counter.

  2. Camera

    Point it at a menu, a notice, a form. It reads the page and tells you what it says.

  3. Study

    Every sentence you have ever needed, kept on the phone and grouped by day, with flashcards built from the ones you starred. Not a textbook deck — the Chinese your own life actually required.

  4. Nearby

    The places on this street, with their Chinese names, the pinyin, and what they are. Big enough to hold up to a taxi driver, and one tap from being spoken aloud or kept in your phrases.

Any phrase can be sent to somebody else. The sentence travels inside the link — so what arrives is the phrase itself, hearable and keepable, not a screenshot of Chinese they cannot use.
