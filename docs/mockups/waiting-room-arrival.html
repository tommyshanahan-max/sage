The waiting room is currently a demotion

The list is not empty for the person on it. It has a card to fill in, a queue position, and three small tests — wr.* in i18n.js is an entire screen of things to do. Being moved up means losing all of that and receiving an app where every button answers soon. The promotion is the worse experience, which is why it feels flat: it is flat.

There is exactly one thing this board owns, and it is who is in it. So that is what the stage should hand over. Not a feature unlocked early — the room itself, with the real names in it. Anybody can put a queue counter on a door. Nobody else can show these fifty people.

Being seen is the reward, not being able to look. Reading the room is nice for ten minutes. Knowing that your page is now in front of members — that the thing you are two fields away from finishing is being read today — is the reason to finish it in the next hour rather than on Thursday. It also fixes the other half of the board: members have had nobody new to look at.

The door opens from the inside. One rule, and it replaces most of the admin work: somebody in the waiting room cannot start a conversation and can always answer one, and answering admits them. A member choosing to write is a stronger signal than anything readable off a card, it arrives without anybody being at a desk, and it means the three lifted each week are not waiting on Tom twice.

Held, not threatened. The seventy-two hours do not change and neither does the sweep back to the list. "Three days or you are out" and "your seat is held for three days" are the same fact, and only one of them is a reason to open the app.

What it would take. The gate in server.js already refuses by method, which is the part that is hard to get right, and it already lets /api/wait/* through. This needs: the arrival screen instead of a toast; the per-card line instead of sawSoon() firing on every press; a lifted person's finished card included in what members browse; and one exception in the gate — a reply on a thread a member opened, which admits them on send. Strings in both languages, as ever.
