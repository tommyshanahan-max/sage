What has to change

Both halves are built. The switch simply refuses the people who need it: notify.js draws nothing unless the reader is a member, and somebody in the waiting room is not one.

  • Let the waiting room subscribe. The gate already allows their own writes; /api/push/on joins /api/wait/* as something they may do.
  • Ask after the page is finished, not on arrival. An ask before they have seen anything is the prompt that gets refused for ever.
  • Then the clock can speak for itself — one buzz at twenty-four hours left, which is the difference between somebody being swept back and somebody remembering.

What this is instead of: automating a personal WeChat account. There is no API for one, the libraries that fake it get the account banned, and on this board the account is the contact list. Not a trade worth making for a notification.
