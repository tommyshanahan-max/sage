Wallet and Pyramid, one app

The wallet says what you have. The pyramid says where it came from. A tab apart, never on one screen.

Why it is shaped like this. Two halves, one tab apart, and neither ever shows the other’s detail. The wallet already exists and does not change. The pyramid is the half nobody else gives them — a picture of their own business they will open every morning, and the reason they do not leave. Every screen carries one level of the tree and never two: a figure, a shape, a row opened.
