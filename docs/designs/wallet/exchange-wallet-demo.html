Tap any screen to jump to it. What you do carries through — a payment you make shows in Activity and in the chat — unless the screen needs the other starting point.

What this is built on

  • Details stay with the provider. Identity checks, bank logins, card numbers and PayPal passwords are only ever typed on Stripe's or PayPal's own page. The Exchange keeps a reference, never the details.
  • Every payment is confirmed on the phone with Face ID or the phone's own passcode.
  • Money waits to be accepted and comes back after 24 hours if it isn't.
  • Two ways to build it: money passing straight between connected accounts through Stripe or PayPal, which carry most of the licensing; or a wallet balance the Exchange holds, which usually needs a licence of its own (a stored value licence in Hong Kong, money transmitter licences in the US).
  • Mainland China: paying in yuan normally runs through WeChat Pay or Alipay, which is why they appear for that region.
  • Fees and limits shown are examples. This is a design, not legal advice.
