The Exchange·the ledger, open to the first 50,000

Everyone up to 50,000 earns points, and the earlier you joined, the more

Each member gets a place number the day they join. The place gives points on a sliding scale, and what they do adds more. If the board reaches 50,000 members, the company makes a proper share offer to everyone on the ledger, allocated by points. Until then, points are a record, not a promise.

A design draft, not a legal document or legal advice. A signed promise of shares to 50,000 people counts as a public share offer, so this version doesn't make one. Points are earned under published rules, and the shares come through a registered or exempt offer only if 50,000 is reached. Anything in brackets is a decision for you and a lawyer.

Why this screen is built this way

    What a lawyer still has to decide

    Opening it to 50,000 people moves the hard part to the day you hit the number. These settle whether that day can happen cleanly.

    The wording of the intentionIt has to be a genuine statement of what the company plans, not a promise dressed as one. Pushed hard enough, a "not a promise" can still be treated as an offer.
    The offer route at 50,000A registered public offer, an exempt offer, or a user share programme like Reddit's in 2024, and in which places.
    Members in mainland ChinaWhether they can take part in an offer from a company outside China, or need a different route or alternative.
    The company and the poolWhich company, where it's registered, what share of it the pool is, and whether later investors can shrink it.
    The place / activity splitHow much is fixed by when you joined, and how much is earned. Fixed-by-place is simplest to defend; earned is the better incentive.
    RecruitingWhether a cap on counted guests is enough that bringing people in isn't the main way to earn, especially under China's pyramid-selling rules.
    Changing the rulesWhether points already earned are protected if the rules change, and how much notice members get.
    A sale before 50,000, and taxWhat ledger holders get, if anything, if the board is sold first; and when shares received for contributing are taxed.
