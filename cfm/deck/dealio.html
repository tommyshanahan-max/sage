how it works ↓

The problem

The paying is easy. The getting is not.

Your client is in China. They can pay anybody in the country in four seconds without leaving a chat.

They cannot pay you. You are a tutor in Sydney, an agent in Auckland, a consultant in London — and between their wallet and your bank is a border built to be difficult.

So it gets done by asking a friend with an overseas account, or not at all.

What it is

A link, and then the money.

You say what you are owed and who it is from. They get one screen and a button.

The difference

Built for the one paying.

Every other product in this corridor is built for the merchant. The payer gets whatever is left.

A Stripe checkout assumes an American reading English. This one assumes somebody already inside WeChat, in a taxi, who has been paying by phone for a decade and does not need any of it explained.

长按二维码识别。 Long-press the code to scan it. — the gesture they already make, said first, in their own language.

It is a small thing and it is the whole product. Nobody else will do it, because it means caring about one corridor.
