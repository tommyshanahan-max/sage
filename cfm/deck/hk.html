Why Hong Kong

You already work both sides.

Half the value of somebody in Hong Kong is that they know people on the other side of a border everybody else has to be introduced across.

That is the whole product. The board is people in China, and with China — and the ones who can move between the two are worth the most in it and get the most out of it.

In eighteen months it stops being a China product at all: Jakarta, Lagos, São Paulo, Dubai. Same room, same sentence.

What it will not do

Three things it does not have.

  1. No feed. Nothing to scroll and nothing to keep up with. You set your line and you are shown the people who answer it.
  2. No cold messages. Nobody can write to you until you have both said you want the same thing. There is no way to reach the room at once.
  3. Nobody gets your contact by being there. Your WeChat moves when you hand it over, one person at a time, and you can take it back.
